About

Built for marketers,
researchers, and creators

commentpull is a tool that lets you extract, filter, and export comments from Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube posts — so you can stop copying comments manually and start focusing on what the data tells you.

Why we built this

Social media comment sections are goldmines of customer feedback, sentiment, and leads — but accessing that data at scale has always been tedious. Copying comments one by one, screenshotting, or wrestling with raw API responses isn't a workflow.

We built commentpull to make extraction fast, structured, and useful. Paste a URL, apply filters, and download clean CSV or Excel data in seconds.

What we support

Facebook

Posts, pages, and public group comments including replies.

Instagram

Business and creator account post comments via the Graph API.

YouTube

Video comments and replies using the YouTube Data API.
