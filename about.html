The story

It started with a giveaway. Someone needed to download all comments from a Facebook post to pick a fair winner — and discovered that the options were either (a) copy-paste everything by hand, (b) pay for an overpriced enterprise tool, or (c) figure out the Graph API yourself.

CommentPull is option (d): open the tool, paste the URL, download comments as Excel or CSV in seconds. No API knowledge required. No subscription. No data sent anywhere you didn't ask it to go.

We added Instagram comment downloading, then YouTube, then filters, a giveaway winner picker, Excel and CSV export, and a token system powered by rewarded ads instead of a paywall — because social media comment extraction should be accessible to everyone, from a solo creator to a brand team.

How it actually works

When you click Extract, your browser makes direct API calls to Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube — not to our servers. Your access token travels from your device to Meta or Google and nowhere else. The comments come back to your browser, get processed in memory, and land in a file on your device.

Our backend only handles three things: your account (email + hashed password), your token balance, and your session. That's it. We've deliberately built a system where we can't see your data even if we wanted to.
