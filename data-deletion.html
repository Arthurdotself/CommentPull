How to remove your commentpull account and all associated data

What data we store

When you create a commentpull account, we store the following data:

  • Email address — used to identify your account
  • Name — displayed in the app interface
  • Password hash — a bcrypt hash, never plain text
  • Scheduled export configurations — including the encrypted YouTube API key needed to run them, and their results
  • Billing reference — your plan and Stripe customer ID, if you subscribed
  • Session tokens — expire automatically after 30 days

Facebook comments collected by the browser extension stay in your browser. We never store your Facebook, Instagram or Google passwords, and we do not store payment card details (Stripe handles those).

Option 1 — Delete via Settings

If you have access to your account, the quickest way to delete your data is directly from the Settings page:

  1. Sign in to your commentpull account
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the "Delete Account" section
  4. Click "Delete my account" and confirm

This permanently deletes your account, scheduled exports, and session data immediately.

Option 2 — Request via email

Send a deletion request to privacy@commentpull.com from the email address linked to your account. We will permanently delete your data within 7 days and send you a confirmation.
