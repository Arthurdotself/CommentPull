How it works

Three simple steps to get your data ready for analysis.

1. Paste URL

Paste any Facebook post, Instagram photo/reel, or YouTube video URL. Platform is detected automatically.

2. Extract

Add your API token and click Extract. All comments, replies, usernames, and timestamps are fetched in real-time.

3. Export

Download your clean data in CSV, Excel, or JSON format — ready for sentiment analysis, reporting, or giveaway draws.
