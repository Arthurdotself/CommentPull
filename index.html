How it works

Facebook
Get your Page Access Token
Go to Meta Graph API Explorer. Select your App → your Page. Add pages_read_engagement permission. Auto-detect fills in your Page ID.
Instagram
Link your Instagram account
Your Instagram must be a Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page. Add instagram_basic + instagram_manage_comments permissions to your token.
Both platforms
Paste your post URLs
Copy the full URL of any post. Add multiple URLs at once with the toggle. Facebook supports pfbid encoded URLs. Instagram supports /p/, /reel/, and /tv/ links.
Both platforms
Download instantly
All comments are fetched with full pagination — no comment limit. Download as Excel (with Summary sheet), CSV, or JSON. Your token and data never touch any server.
