Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how commentpull ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services at commentpull.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you sign up.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and general interaction with the service, collected via Google Analytics.
  • API tokens — access tokens you provide to connect your social media accounts. These are stored securely and used solely to perform extractions you request.
  • Support messages — any information you include when contacting us through our support form.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and improve the commentpull service.
  • To authenticate you and manage your account.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To understand how the product is used so we can improve it.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Service providers that help us operate the platform (e.g., hosting, analytics, payment processors), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. API tokens and social media data

Access tokens you provide are used exclusively to fetch comment data on your behalf. We do not store raw comment data beyond what is needed to display and export your results. Tokens are encrypted at rest.

5. Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication and Google Analytics cookies for usage analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.

6. Data retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time by contacting us at info@commentpull.com.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS, encrypted storage of sensitive data, and access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children's privacy

commentpull is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@commentpull.com.
